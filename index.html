📊 About the Project


The IoT-Based Smart Weather Monitoring Dashboard is designed to monitor important environmental parameters such as temperature, humidity, wind speed, atmospheric pressure and visibility.


The dashboard provides a simple and user-friendly interface for displaying weather information in real time. It can be extended in the future by connecting sensors such as DHT11, BMP280 and ESP32.
